Chowmein

Home

Description

Chowmein is a popular noodle dish made by stir-frying cooked noodles with vegetables, seasonings, and sometimes meat. It is commonly prepared with ingredients such as cabbage, carrot, onion, capsicum, garlic, and spring onion.

The noodles are first boiled and then stir-fried with the vegetables and sauces over high heat. Soy sauce, chili sauce, salt, and pepper are often added for flavor, and the finished chowmein is served hot

Ingredients

  • Noodels
  • Onion
  • Cabbage
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles: Cook the noodles until tender, then drain them.
  2. Prepare the vegetables: Chop the vegetables, garlic, ginger, and onion.
  3. Stir-fry: Heat oil and fry the onion, garlic, ginger, and vegetables.
  4. Add the noodles: Add the cooked noodles, soy sauce, chili sauce, salt, and pepper
